# Brief

> **Sample project — synthetic data, not client work.** "Northwind Home Goods" is a fictional
> brand. Every order, customer and ad-spend figure in this folder was generated by
> `src/generate_data.py` with a fixed random seed.

## Client request (synthetic)

> "We're a DTC e-commerce brand (Northwind Home Goods). Here's 18 months of order exports from
> Shopify and ad spend from Meta/Google. Give us a monthly performance report: revenue, orders,
> AOV, repeat-customer rate, cohort retention, channel ROAS/CAC, top/bottom products, and 3–5
> concrete recommendations. We need an Excel workbook our ops manager can refresh, plus a
> one-page summary for the founder."

## Inputs (as "received")

| File | Source it imitates | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `data/raw/shopify_orders_export.csv` | Shopify order export, one row per line item | USD + CAD, discounts, refunds, and real-world mess: duplicated rows, missing emails, negative quantities, mixed timestamp formats/time zones, test orders |
| `data/raw/ad_spend_daily.csv` | Meta Ads + Google Ads daily exports, merged | One row per day × channel × campaign, USD |
| `data/raw/products.csv` | Product catalog | SKU, name, category, unit cost |
| `data/raw/fx_rates_monthly.csv` | Finance team's monthly CAD→USD rates | Used to convert CAD orders |

Period: 1 Apr 2025 – 30 Sep 2026 (18 months). Store reporting time zone: America/New_York.

## Deliverables

1. `deliverables/Northwind_Performance_Report.xlsx` — Summary KPIs, Monthly, Cohorts (heatmap),
   Channels, Products, Data Quality Log, README (how to refresh).
2. `deliverables/executive_summary.md` — one page for the founder; every number ties out to the workbook.
3. `deliverables/*.png` — 4 charts.
4. Independent QA script (`src/verify.py`) that recomputes headline KPIs from the raw files by a
   separate code path (standard library only, no pandas) and checks them against the workbook.

## Definitions agreed for this report

- **Gross sales** = quantity × unit price, converted to USD.
- **Net revenue** = gross sales − discounts − refunds (excludes shipping and tax).
- **Orders** = distinct valid order IDs (test and cancelled orders excluded).
- **AOV** = net revenue ÷ orders.
- **Repeat-customer rate (monthly)** = share of that month's purchasing customers who had ordered before.
- **Cohort retention** = share of customers first acquired in month M who ordered again in month M+k.
- **ROAS** = net revenue from orders last-click attributed to the channel ÷ channel spend.
- **CAC** = channel spend ÷ new customers whose first order was attributed to that channel.
